# Sanaa Heritage Crafts

Welcome to **Sanaa Heritage Crafts**, a digital platform that showcases authentic African handmade crafts and promotes the creativity, culture, and skills of local artisans.

## About the Project

Sanaa Heritage Crafts is a website designed to display and market locally manufactured arts and crafts. The platform allows visitors to explore unique handmade products, learn about their cultural significance, and connect with the craft business.

## Features

- Beautiful showcase of handmade crafts
- Product gallery with craft collections
- Information about the founder and brand story
- Contact section for customer inquiries
- Responsive design for desktop and mobile users

## Purpose

The goal of Sanaa Heritage Crafts is to preserve African artistic heritage while helping artisans reach more customers through digital technology.

## Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
